# Acceptance criteria

1. Linear heating, isolated conductive relaxation, pure radiative cooling and radiative equilibrium agree with analytic references.
2. Piecewise heating energy and final temperature match hand calculation.
3. Fixture energy residual and refined convergence meet stated thresholds; emissivity response has expected sign.
4. Invalid units/capacity/emissivity/asymmetric links and oversized sample bounds reject; repeated outputs match.
5. Eight tests, Ruff/format/pip checks, generated reports and visually inspected plots pass; physical validation explicitly absent.

All criteria must be executed locally or explicitly listed as unresolved. Reviewer acceptance is bounded by this profile.
